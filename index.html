Research Interests

👁 Computer Vision & Pattern Recognition

Developing interpretable, efficient, and scalable visual intelligence systems with emphasis on person re-identification, fine-grained visual recognition, and crowd analysis.

🤖 Deep Learning & Multimodal AI

Designing novel deep learning architectures for high-order feature interactions, multimodal fusion, and real-time inference in autonomous and intelligent systems.

🚨 Visual Anomaly Detection

Building training-less and adaptive frameworks for anomaly detection in crowd scenes and surveillance environments using lightweight, resource-efficient methods.

🏥 Smart Healthcare Applications

Leveraging vision-based sensing for unobtrusive symptom monitoring — including Parkinson's disease assessment and infant pulse rate estimation — to enable remote clinical care.

🛸 Autonomous Systems & Robotics

Integrating trustworthy AI principles into drone-based triage systems, UAV-guided navigation, and traffic scene analysis for safety-critical real-world deployments.

🔬 Human Activity Recognition

Developing pose-estimation and motion analysis techniques for gesture-based interaction, activity classification, and mobility assessment in naturalistic environments.
